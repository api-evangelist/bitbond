Qivalis, a stablecoin from 37 European banks, creates a new euro settlement layer for on-chain transactions. For corporate treasurers, its true value is unlocked by tokenizing assets like commercial paper using the ERC-1400 standard to enable instant, 24/7 Delivery-versus-Payment settlement.
