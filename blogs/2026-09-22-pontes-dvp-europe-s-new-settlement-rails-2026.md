The Eurosystem's Pontes platform is now live, enabling tokenized assets to settle against central bank money via atomic Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP). This eliminates settlement risk for institutional markets, a principle also available for OTC trades using smart contracts to create trustless, on-chain DvP.
