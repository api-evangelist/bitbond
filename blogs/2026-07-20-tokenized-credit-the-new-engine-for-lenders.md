DeFi protocols are proving the model for on-chain credit, but the real opportunity is for traditional lenders to tokenize their own loan books. This unlocks liquidity, improves capital efficiency, and expands investor access by turning illiquid debt into tradable digital assets, managed via institutional-grade tokenization platforms.
