The emergence of fixed-term lending in DeFi, exemplified by protocols like Morpho Midnight, provides the blueprint for bringing institutional-grade credit on-chain. This model enables the tokenization of traditional fixed-income assets like corporate bonds, offering predictable yields, automated servicing, and enhanced liquidity for issuers.
