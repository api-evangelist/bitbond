The new SEC 'Reg Crypto' regulations demand issuers maintain strict operational control over token sales. Managing a sale effectively involves pre-configuring compliant parameters like whitelists and pricing, monitoring live progress from a central dashboard, and handling post-sale distributions methodically. Platforms like Bitbond's Token Tool provide the necessary controls.
